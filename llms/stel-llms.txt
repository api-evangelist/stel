# Stel

> Stel Life provides cellular-connected remote patient monitoring (RPM) infrastructure for healthcare. Cellular-enabled vitals hubs and connected medical devices (blood pressure monitors, pulse oximeters, glucometers, thermometers, scales) transmit vital-sign readings to electronic health record (EHR) systems without WiFi, broadband, a smartphone, or app setup — serving elderly, low-income, rural, and low-tech-literacy populations across the care continuum from wellness and chronic-care management to hospital-at-home. Backed by Bloomberg Beta.
